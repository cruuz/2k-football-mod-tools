## Interpretation limits

Table masks are inputs to category routines, not the complete resulting credit. The common drill routine `0x2A9AB0` also uses the position mask for full and seven-on-seven drills, native random draws, fatigue, morale and current ratings. Initial losses, rounding and the native positive caps (+5 for position attributes, +3 otherwise) explain why equal booked hours do not promise identical changes for different players. Getters and setters retain retail clamps.

TE position 9 passes subtype 11 and its five activity rows (54..58) have the same intensity, masks and flags as HB (39..43) and FB (44..48). DB subtype 7 tests only position 4 in retail and drops FS 5 and SS 6. The patch admits all three. No TE-specific cause is asserted.
