### Method warning: "zero references" from the function ledger is not evidence

Raised by G15 and it invalidates a class of earlier conclusions, so it is
recorded here rather than buried in that row.

`research/functions/*/functions.tsv` under-reports callers. Ghidra truncated
functions that open `mflr r12; bl __savegprlr` — the helpers at
`0x84BD6DD0-0x84BD6DFC` end in `blr`, so Ghidra closed the function there.
**6,827 of 21,347 APF ledger rows (32.0%) are recorded as size 8**, and 7,847 of
17,083 `.pdata`-matched rows are shorter in the ledger than in `.pdata`. A
`caller_count` of 0 in that corpus therefore means "Ghidra did not see it", not
"nothing calls it".

Two concrete corrections already produced by re-running the scan properly:

- The fantasy priority table `0x820F4B70` is **not** an orphan. It is
  materialised at `0x84A5B944 lis r11,0x820F` + `0x84A5B94C addi r28,r11,0x4B70`,
  and again at `0x84A5C948`/`0x84A5C950`; `0x820053F0` likewise at
  `0x84680378`/`0x84680388`. The audit's
  `conventional_lis_addi_or_ori_materializations: []` and
  `direct_ghidra_references_to_table: []` are truncation artifacts.
- NFL 2K5 `FUN_0036F830` is not reached by virtual dispatch. A full-file 32-bit
  LE scan finds zero data words holding it, and an `E8`/`E9` rel32 scan finds two
  ordinary direct callers.

Before asserting any negative about references, run the byte-level `E8`/`E9`
(x86) or opcode-18/16 (PowerPC) branch scan over the whole `.text`, and search
the image for the address as a data word. Do not rely on the ledger's `callers`
column alone.
