# INFERRED: 2026 defensive coordinators and scheme lineage

INFERRED: Staff names and prior jobs are sourced to each official biography below, cross-checked against [ESPN, current staffs, page 74, September 9](https://g.espncdn.com/s/ffldraftkit/26/NFLDK2026_CS_ClayProjections2026.pdf#page=74). Scheme families and odd/even base labels are coaching-lineage interpretations, not measured alignment rates. A DC title does not guarantee that coach calls the defense. The baseline column explicitly identifies the prior job for new coordinators; assistant-staff numbers are team tendencies, never personal playcalling percentages.

PROVED OFFLINE: Every percentage below is computed by [aggregate_defense.py](aggregate_defense.py) from the hashed [2025 release inputs, counts, denominators and coverage labels](defense_tendencies_2025.json). Sources: [nflverse participation](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/tag/pbp_participation), [PBP](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/tag/pbp), [public FTN charting](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/tag/ftn_charting). Attribution: nflverse / FTN; derived charting data retains CC-BY-SA-4.0. No premium FTN access or unpublished numbers are claimed.

PROVED OFFLINE: Eligible snaps are regular-season run/pass plays excluding kneels and spikes. Nickel means exactly five DB positions; dime exactly six, among known eleven-player personnel. These include run snaps. Coverage and rush rates use QB dropbacks only, including sacks and scrambles, with separate known denominators. A missing coverage or zero/sentinel rusher count is excluded, not treated as zone or no blitz. Blitz means five or more counted rushers, not any off-ball rusher. [Participation dictionary](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_participation.html), [FTN subset dictionary](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_ftn_charting.html).

PROVED OFFLINE: The public FTN subset has no defensive shell, front or pressure-concept fields. Participation has coverage labels and aggregate rush counts but no rusher identities, alignment landmarks or pre-snap shell. Actual single-high/two-high, simulated-pressure and creeper rates are unavailable (NA) for every row. Single-family means Cover 1 + 3; split-family means Cover 2 + 2-Man + 4 + 6 + 9, divided by all known coverage labels. These post-snap family proxies are deliberately not called measured pre-snap shells. Cover 0 and combo/blown labels explain why family columns do not sum to 100.

DESIGN: The menu fit uses the available coverage-family counts and blitz rates. It merges Cover 9 into split-field Cover 6 because the encodable library has a fixed split-field orientation. Nickel/dime and aligned-front rates are research references, not promised menu-based personnel control. The engine responds to offensive personnel and game state. SD has no accessible Western Michigan coverage dataset; its real-rate cells remain NA and its design uses the disclosed 2025 LAC continuity fallback.
