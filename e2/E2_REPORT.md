## The playbook conflict and exact repair design

**PROVED OFFLINE:** `e2/audit_offline.py` executes the actual retail default-book formatter at `0x628D0`, including native `sprintf`, after each moment's real import and match copy. For h1 it executes the real Team Select next handler for all 75 descriptors. [Reproducer](audit_offline.py), [receipt and source hashes](evidence/native_books.json).

**PROVED OFFLINE:** The mechanism is:

1. Historic import at `0x2D17B0` loads the selected historic file into a spare team object. Its category is 4 at `team+0x128`.
2. The importer matches franchise art code at `team+0x10C` and copies that franchise's label pointer into `team+0x110` at `0x2D18D9`. h1 duplicates this rule. The displayed abbreviation at `team+0x108`, such as `SF81`, is a separate field.
3. At `0x628D0`, argument 1 resolves the home match team via `0x61C50`, argument 0 the away team via `0x61C60`. Optional manually selected books have an earlier override path through `0x775F0`/`0x77720`.
4. The default path at `0x62A1B` reads `[team+0x110]`; `0x62A21` reads `[label+4]`. `0x62A2D` supplies the `%s-pb.iff` format at `0xE61084`, and `0x62A32` calls `0x4A400`. Output buffers are `0xB307D0` home and `0xB30810` away.
5. Thus it resolves **per match side from a shared franchise label**. It is neither a book table indexed by the imported spare slot nor a historical-season table. Numeric team identity alone does not select a stock book. The book contains offense and defense together.

**PROVED OFFLINE:** Every traced filename is among the modern pack targets. `apply_packs_to_archive` looks up the fixed outer entry from `BOOK_ENTRIES`, compiles the next pack against the pending replacement of that same entry, and writes it in place. ## Era rules: actual differences, expressibility and design

**INFERRED:** Use the football **season**, not the calendar year of the playoff. NFL two-point scoring begins in 1994, while the AFL used it throughout 1960-1969. Thus the 1968 Heidi game must allow two points even though the other early NFL moments must not. Pre-1994 NFL non-kick tries could still score one point; a correct implementation must change scoring and AI, not merely hide every pass/run try. [Hall of Fame history](https://www.profootballhof.com/news/2-point-conversion-turns-30-years-old), [1993 NFL rule summary](https://www.profootballhof.com/football-history/football-history/1980-1999/1994).

**INFERRED:** The kickoff moved from 40 to 35 in 1974, to 30 in 1994, and back to 35 in 2011. Free-kick touchbacks were at 20 through 2015 and 25 from 2016; scrimmage-kick touchbacks remain 20. PAT kick snaps move from 2 to 15 in 2015, alongside live defensive try returns. [1974 chronology](https://www.profootballhof.com/football-history/football-history/1960-1979/1962), [1994 chronology](https://www.profootballhof.com/football-history/football-history/1980-1999/1994), [2011 kickoff / 2016 touchback](https://www.nfl.com/news/sean-payton-looking-forward-to-safer-exciting-kickoffs-0ap3000000934415), [2015 rulebook](https://operations.nfl.com/media/1807/2015_nfl_rule_book_final.pdf), [2016 rulebook](https://edge-operations.nfl.com/media/2224/2016-nfl-rulebook.pdf).

**INFERRED:** Regular-season overtime starts in 1974. Modified sudden death starts with the 2010 postseason and the 2012 regular season: an opening touchdown ends the game, an opening field goal does not. Regular-season overtime shortens to ten minutes in 2017. Both sides get an opportunity even after an opening touchdown in the postseason starting in the 2022 season. Postseason periods remain 15 minutes and continue as required. [Hall of Fame OT history](https://www.profootballhof.com/news/nfl-s-first-experiment-with-ot), [NFL OT chronology](https://nfl-ops-prod-umbraco-author.azurewebsites.net/rules-officiating/featured-rules/overtime-rules/), [2024 NFL record book](https://operations.nfl.com/media/hfjn4oid/2024-record-fact-book-incl-supplemental.pdf).

**INFERRED:** Super Bowl LVIII uses **2023** rules: 35-yard conventional kickoff, 25-yard free-kick touchback, fair catches behind the 25 spotted at the 25, and postseason both-possession overtime. It does not use the 2024 dynamic kickoff. Thirteen Seconds uses **2021** postseason modified sudden death, so Kansas City's opening overtime touchdown ends it. [2023 rulebook](https://operations.nfl.com/media/rwnj5upg/2023-rulebook_final.pdf), [2021 playoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_AFC_Divisional_playoff_game_(Buffalo%E2%80%93Kansas_City)), [LVIII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Bowl_LVIII).
