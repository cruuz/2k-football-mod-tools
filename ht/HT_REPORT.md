## Ratings and the held writer

**DESIGN:** For a player without a same-slot retail candidate, `era_ratings` supplies a documented prior: exact retail position, target's five-year era, at least eight candidate donor observations if available, widening symmetrically by five years until enough exist. A reserve uses the component median order statistic; an explicitly sourced starter uses the 75th-percentile order statistic. Rounding uses Python's deterministic nearest-integer rule on `(n-1)*q`. Style channels come from a real donor nearest the resulting vector, not interpolation. Each proposal records era bounds, donor count, quantile and basis. [Implementation](../mod_editor/core/nfl2k5_historic_rosters.py).
