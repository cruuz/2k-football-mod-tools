**PROVED OFFLINE:** Retail fields were decoded from the pinned resources. All 53 slots, 28 rating bytes, sizes, equipment and source candidates are in [retail_audit.json](evidence/retail_audit.json). The summary below is generated by [summarize.py](tools/summarize.py).

**INFERRED:** A unique number plus compatible position is a candidate identity, not proof that the developer intended that person. "Missing" means absent from those unique candidates. Ambiguous and unmatched slots must not be silently called proper players. Named stars below means names in the supplied article's Pro Bowl/All-Pro infobox fields; this is not an exhaustive fame ranking.

**DESIGN:** Drafts retain unresolved rows and are not playable rosters. Fewer than 53 source people or a scarce position is a data/model gap, not authority to duplicate a person or borrow from another season. No listed starters means the parser found no supported explicit starter list, not that the team had none.
