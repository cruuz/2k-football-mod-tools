# b72-s11 full-mark fit method

This is an offline logo-fit correction on base c3b3b53a2. The enlarged s10 wells, wing ramps, scores, plate, housing, palettes and runtime writer are unchanged. Only the explicit per-team fits in the layout and matching accent metadata change. The native texture remains 64 by 64 and every appended component keeps its size.

The s10 audit inspected alpha after the oversized artwork had already been pasted into that cell. That denominator could not detect missing strokes. `fit.py` reconstructs the complete transform first, using the source PNG's nonzero alpha bounding box, its aspect, the existing team-specific horizontal compensation, and the integer dimensions used by the unchanged native texture builder. It measures both original source alpha and the complete filtered mark, including coloured ink. No RGB-brightness threshold can hide Washington's burgundy strokes or another dark logo.

Every nonzero source pixel contributes its original alpha weight. All four corners of its transformed footprint must fit within the texture window and the rounded outer wing. Binary ink-pixel counts are also retained. The filtered-alpha denominator is taken before any clipping paste. The runtime result's alpha is checked against an independently reconstructed paste. The existing low-alpha tail suppression and P8 colour quantization are unchanged, and are not mislabelled as boundary cropping.

Zero crop tolerance applies to every primary mark, including circles, shields, lettering and asymmetric artwork. A complete transparent texel border guards texture sampling and the rounded wing corners. The author first checks each existing fit, then selects its largest safe size while retaining its horizontal proportion. A native-size search skips duplicate integer transforms. Because the source marks are tightly cropped and normalized into a fixed cell, many maxima share the same height limit. Their widths, source aspect, initial losses and reduction factors still come from their own artwork. The safe result is verified individually; a common zoom value is not treated as evidence of safety.

For the four teams with same-matchup broadcast references, `search.py` measures 80 safe size/position candidates over both native aspects. It changes only logo zoom and position. The chosen Chiefs horizontal offset preserves the full arrowhead and improves its reference alignment. Every trial and rejected smaller size is retained in `search_trials.json`. `selected_fits.json` records the reproducible selections; `fit.py` applies them only after the full-ink gate passes. No model preference overrides an ink or residual measurement.

`audit.py` executes each of the 52 roster slots in both native projections. For each of the 32 primary NFL marks it maps the complete source footprints through the actual executed logo and wing quads at both ends. The player projection uses the same 448-line HUD and 617-pixel apparent bar width as s10. Both weighted and binary survival fractions must be exactly one. The 20 historical, all-star and user slots retain their existing neutral fallback; they have no new authored primary-logo PNG in this bundle. Their labels and native tints are still checked among the 104 slot/aspect cases.

`all_teams_43.png` and `all_teams_169.png` show every roster slot at player scale. Six sheets compare s10 and s11 for WAS-LAC, LV-HOU and the circular PIT-TEN marks. The WAS sheets also retain the four supplied disc r captures with their actual provenance. The Raiders sheet includes the supplied broadcast normalized to the same bar width. No before is relabelled as a different matchup or aspect. Open the sheets at 100 percent.

The residual implementation is copied from s10 without changing its thresholds, ROIs, references, display size or 2-by-2 player-pixel core rule. Fresh s10 renders are compared with the recorded s10 results, then s11 is measured in the same two matchups and both aspects. The logo residual is the existing white-ink occupancy proxy; it is separate from the all-colour, complete-source containment gate. Logo and wing ROIs overlap. The search sums their proxies only to rank candidates, never as a physical area. Final acceptance separately asserts that each required feature preserves its s10 gain.

The new standalone regression intercepts the real Pillow paste, compares full and surviving alpha, and deliberately rejects the original Washington, Texans, Raiders and Steelers fits. The existing wide-mark test now checks the complete Chiefs silhouette's 1.59 aspect instead of the cropped 1.74 aspect, with the same 0.12 tolerance and 1.4 lower bound. No label, native-byte, footprint, colour or integrity check is relaxed.

These results establish geometric containment and bounded native CPU/software-raster behaviour. They do not establish GPU/display calibration, the survival of every fine detail after low-resolution sampling, perceptual identity at a glance, or an in-game outcome. No emulator, disc build or release action is part of this job.
