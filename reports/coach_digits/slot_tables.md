# Coach Edwards slot measurements

Read-only retail metadata and deterministic synthetic art. Boxes are half-open `(left, top, right, bottom)`. Registration uses alpha >=16; opaque means alpha >=240. All byte sizes below exclude the fixed outer wrapper and include the VC-LZ stream framing. Every stored TXTR palette allocates 256 BGRA8888 entries; colour counts are the distinct entries actually sampled, not that capacity.
